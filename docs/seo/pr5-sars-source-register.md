## Deliberate qualifications

- No claim that a payment arrangement is automatically approved or always lasts six months.
- No fixed compromise processing time is stated.
- No claim that debt age alone creates compromise eligibility.
- No old “ADR1” terminology is asserted.
- Objection and appeal deadlines are described as general rules, with qualifications for reasons/late filing.
- Objection/appeal is not described as automatically suspending payment.
- Compromise supporting documents are described as published examples rather than a universally exhaustive list.
- Enforcement measures are described as possible collection measures, not as inevitable steps in every case.
