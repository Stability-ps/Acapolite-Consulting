## Deliberate limits

- No tax-season filing deadline is hard-coded because annual filing dates change.
- No claim is made that every taxpayer must file an ITR12; the page describes assistance for taxpayers who need a return filed.
- No CIPC deregistration timeline is stated.
- No accounting standard, assurance level or statutory audit claim is made unless separately agreed with the client.
- No turnaround-time, price, guarantee or success-rate claim is made.
