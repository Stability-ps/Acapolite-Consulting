## Deliberate limits

- The page does not say turnover alone always determines registration; it refers to taxable supplies and the applicable rules.
- The page does not promise that a VAT refund will be released or give a fixed refund timeframe.
- The page does not claim that every delayed refund is caused by verification or audit.
- No PAYE money page is created in this PR.
- No VAT rate or filing deadline is hard-coded where it is unnecessary to the service intent.
